Strong communication skills are essential for a child’s development—and with ABA techniques, parents can play a key role in fostering those skills at home. Whether your child is verbal or nonverbal, the following strategies can help encourage more meaningful interactions in everyday situations.

### 1. **Modeling Language**

Speak clearly and frequently. If your child points to juice, you can say, “You want juice!” This helps them associate words with actions and needs.

### 2. **Use Mand Training**

This ABA method focuses on teaching children to request what they want. Start by prompting the child to say or sign for something they enjoy, like “ball” or “cookie,” and reinforce it immediately.

### 3. **Provide Communication Choices**

Offer two options verbally or visually (e.g., “Do you want apples or bananas?”) to give your child the opportunity to express a preference, building independence and confidence.

### 4. **Narrate Your Activities**

Talk about what you or your child are doing throughout the day. “Now we’re putting on socks. One sock, two socks!” This provides natural language exposure tied to real actions.

### 5. **Celebrate Attempts**

Even if your child doesn't say a word perfectly, respond enthusiastically to their effort. This positive feedback reinforces their willingness to try communicating again.

---

Every interaction is an opportunity to build communication. These everyday ABA techniques can help turn daily routines into powerful moments of learning and connection.
