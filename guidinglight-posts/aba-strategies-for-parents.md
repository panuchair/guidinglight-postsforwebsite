Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) isn't just for therapists—it can empower parents too. By integrating simple ABA principles into your daily routine, you can help reinforce positive behaviors and support your child’s growth in a natural setting. Here are five practical strategies any parent can start using today:

### 1. **Positive Reinforcement**

Praise or reward your child when they complete a task or behave appropriately. For example, saying “Great job putting your toys away!” reinforces that action and encourages repetition.

### 2. **Clear Instructions**

Give short, direct commands. Instead of “Can you clean up?”, say “Please put the blocks in the box.” This reduces confusion and increases compliance.

### 3. **Visual Schedules**

Many children benefit from seeing what comes next. Use a simple picture-based schedule to show steps for daily routines like brushing teeth or getting ready for bed.

### 4. **Break Tasks into Steps**

This is known as task chaining. If your child struggles with a task like handwashing, break it down: turn on water → wet hands → get soap → scrub → rinse → dry.

### 5. **Use First/Then Language**

“First eat your lunch, then we’ll play outside.” This structure sets clear expectations and gives your child something to look forward to.

---

Consistency is key. Using these strategies throughout the day can create structure and help your child feel more confident and understood.
